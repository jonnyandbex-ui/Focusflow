Answers

Frequently asked questions.

Everything you need to know about getting started with Focu Flow.

Is Focu Flow completely free? +
Yes! Focu Flow is fully free to use in your browser. All features including the focus timer, weekly planner generator, subject manager, and progress charts are available without paywalls.
Where is my study data stored? +
Your data is stored privately in your browser's local storage. Your study records, subjects, and notes remain on your own device and are never sold or sent to remote advertising servers.
How does the Focus Timer log progress? +
Whenever you complete a focus interval (or reset after studying for at least one minute), your study minutes automatically register under your active subject, immediately updating your daily goal and 7-day activity graph.
Can I use Focu Flow on mobile or tablet? +
Yes. Focu Flow is responsive and includes a dedicated bottom navigation bar and mobile viewports tailored for smartphones and iPads.
